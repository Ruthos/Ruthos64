# Horos icons

These PNG files are rendered from the icon artwork of the
[Horos project](https://github.com/horosproject/horos)
(`Horos/Resources/Icons`), which is licensed under the GNU Lesser General
Public License v3.0, the same licence as Ruthos. Vector (PDF) originals were
rasterised at 128 px; bitmap originals (TIFF, ICNS) were resized to 128 px.

Apple and Horos trademark artwork (the Photos and Preferences icons and the
Horos logos) is deliberately not included.

## Ruthos' own icons

`import_images.png`, `add_images.png` and `report_custom.png` were made for
Ruthos (supplied by the project owner) and are licensed with the rest of the
project under the LGPL-3.0. Their black backgrounds were keyed out and they
were scaled to 128 px.
